# Maggie O'Neill Business & Training Centre — Image Library

**Common visual themes:** warm oak slat feature walls, lots of real and faux greenery (trailing plants, living-wall panels, a greenery ceiling canopy), white walls, charcoal/black chairs with grey or white tables, a purple neon 'three people' logo, bold colourful typographic art prints, and Scouse-dialect humour in the signage.
