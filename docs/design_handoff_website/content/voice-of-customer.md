# Voice of customer (Sept 2026)

Source: Google reviews (230 total as of 28 Sept 2026; 76 with text analysed), CRM conversations, Site Kit (30 Jun to 27 Sep 2026). Strategy built on this: brand/Brand Strategy.dc.html.

## What delights them (share of written reviews)
1. Being welcomed and looked after: 66%. Staff named in 22% (Patrick, Tania; Liam is a volunteer). Spell it Tania.
2. Everything works and is ready: 35%.
3. How it feels inside (calm, homely, the diffuser scent, flowers): 32%.
4. Easy to reach 23%, parking 19%.
5. Clean: 23%.
6. Refreshments included: 20%.
7. Easy from booking to the day: 12%.
Price comes up ~9%. Community/charity ~4%, Maggie's Meals 0%.

## Mix
Training/courses 37% of written reviews, meetings 16%, coworking 8%. Top customers: Amore, AFG, Adoption in Merseyside, Livv, TACT. 20+ care companies in CRM.

## Questions asked before booking
Price/day rate · availability · screen/AV · tea and coffee (included? where?) · free parking · layout/tables · half vs full day · cancellation (multi-day) · "can I speak to a person" · do I bring equipment/HDMI.
