04 · Virtual office growth plan

The service with no ceiling

Room hire tops out when the rooms are full, the car park is full or the offices are let. A virtual office client needs a folder in a filing cabinet. Every new client adds revenue without taking space from anyone, so this is a lead service with its own goal: to be the virtual office Merseyside businesses choose. We start from 38 clients, and the process can handle 150.
